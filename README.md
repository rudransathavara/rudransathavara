### 02 / ReceiptFlow

A finance application in early development designed to help small businesses, accountants, and individuals manage receipts, track VAT, and understand their spending.

The goal is to make expense tracking easier by bringing receipt information and spending summaries into one place.

**Key features**
- Receipt uploads and data extraction
- Automatic VAT calculation
- Receipt verification when information is unclear
- Monthly spending by category
- Planned bank statement support

**Status:** Early development. Basic features are being developed and tested.

The project also documents product decisions, development progress, testing, and planned improvements.
